Brethren Bible Reading Club (BBRC) Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

1. Introduction

The Brethren Bible Reading Club (BBRC) is committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of individuals who use its Bible Reading Application (the "App").

The App provides access to daily Bible reading plans, Bible passages, reading check-ins, reading progress records and automated reading reminders. It also provides access to the BBRC community.

This Privacy Policy explains how BBRC collects, uses, stores, protects and manages personal information in connection with the use of the App. BBRC is the data controller responsible for personal information processed through the App.

By registering for or using the App, users acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

2. Information we collect

BBRC collects information necessary to provide the App's services and support its reading accountability activities.

a. Personal and account information

During registration, users provide:

  • Full name;
  • Email address; and
  • Phone number.

This information is used for account creation, identification, authentication and relevant club communications.

b. Bible reading and activity information

The App records:

  • Daily Bible reading check-ins;
  • Dates on which readings are marked as completed;
  • Reading progress and streaks; and
  • Weekly reading totals.

This information enables users to monitor their reading consistency and allows the club to coordinate its reading accountability activities.

c. Notification information

The App processes device notification tokens to facilitate the delivery of reading reminders and relevant notifications.

Users may turn reminders off at any time using the Reminders switch in their Profile, or disable notifications through their device settings.

d. Account deletion information

When deleting their account, users may optionally give a reason for leaving. Any reason given is kept with the account and erased together with it.

Information we do not collect

The App does not collect users' location, contacts, photos or payment information. The App contains no advertising and uses no third-party analytics.

3. How we use personal information

Information collected through the App is used for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing user accounts;
  • Verifying user identity and facilitating secure sign-in;
  • Providing daily Bible reading plans and Bible passages;
  • Recording and monitoring reading activities;
  • Calculating reading progress and weekly accountability records;
  • Facilitating communication relating to club activities;
  • Providing access to the BBRC community; and
  • Sending reading reminders and relevant notifications.

BBRC does not sell users' personal information or use it for advertising purposes. Personal information is not used for purposes unrelated to the operation and administration of the App.

4. Access to personal information

Personal information is accessed and processed only where reasonably necessary for the operation, maintenance and administration of the App.

Authorised club administrators may access relevant membership information and reading accountability records for legitimate administrative purposes.

BBRC does not make members' personal account information or individual reading records publicly available through the App.

5. Third-party services

BBRC uses selected third-party service providers to support the operation and delivery of the App's services. These include:

  • Render and Neon: infrastructure and database hosting services.
  • Expo: application infrastructure and notification delivery services.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivery of push notifications.
  • Brevo: delivery of one-time sign-in codes by email.
  • YouVersion: provision of the Bible text displayed within the App.

These service providers process information only as necessary to provide their respective services.

YouVersion does not receive users' account details or Bible reading records. However, when the App displays Bible text, it loads styling resources from YouVersion's servers, so certain technical information, such as the device's IP address, may be visible to YouVersion.

BBRC does not sell or disclose users' personal information to advertisers.

6. International data transfers

Some of these service providers store and process data on servers located outside Nigeria. Where personal information is transferred outside Nigeria, BBRC relies on providers that maintain appropriate security measures, in line with applicable data protection laws, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

7. Community access and WhatsApp

The App provides a feature that allows users to access the Brethren Bible Reading Club's WhatsApp community. Selecting this feature redirects users to WhatsApp, an independent third-party messaging platform.

Participation in the WhatsApp community is voluntary and subject to WhatsApp's own terms of service and privacy policy.

Users should be aware that information such as their phone numbers and profile details may be visible to other participants in the WhatsApp community, depending on the platform's features and individual privacy settings.

BBRC does not control how WhatsApp processes personal information on its platform.

8. Data security

BBRC implements reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction.

Information transmitted between the App and its servers is protected through encrypted HTTPS connections.

Access to personal information is restricted to authorised persons and service providers who require such access for legitimate operational purposes.

Although reasonable security measures are maintained, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data retention and account deletion

BBRC keeps account information and reading records for as long as the user remains a member.

Users may delete their account at any time through the Delete My Account feature in their Profile. Club administrators may also deactivate or remove an account for legitimate membership reasons.

When an account is deleted, the user is signed out on every device, and the account and its reading records are removed from the App, including from weekly reports. The account then enters a 30-day recovery period, during which its information is retained so that the account can be restored if the user changes their mind. To request recovery, users should contact BBRC at the email address below within the 30 days.

If the account is not recovered within the 30-day period, the account and its associated personal information, including individual reading records, notification information and any reason given for leaving, are permanently deleted.

Certain non-personal administrative records, such as reading plans created for the club, may be retained where necessary for the continued operation of the club. Where applicable, identifying information associated with a deleted account is removed.

10. Privacy rights

Subject to applicable data protection laws, users have the right to:

  • Request access to their personal information;
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • Request deletion of their personal information;
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and
  • Raise concerns or complaints regarding the handling of their personal information, including with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Requests concerning personal information may be directed to BBRC through the contact details provided below.

11. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over. Users under 18 should use the App with the permission of a parent or guardian.

BBRC does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If BBRC becomes aware that such information has been provided, it will be deleted.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

BBRC reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the App, its services or applicable legal requirements. Any revised policy will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

Users are encouraged to review the Privacy Policy periodically.
