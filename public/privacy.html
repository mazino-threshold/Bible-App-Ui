BBRC Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

BBRC is a Bible reading app for members of our reading club. It shows the daily reading plan, lets members check in when they have read, and sends reading reminders. This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your full name, email address and phone number, which you enter when you register.
  • Reading check-ins: the days you mark a reading as done.
  • Notification token: a device token from Google Firebase / Expo so we can send you reading reminders. You can turn reminders off with the Reminders switch in your Profile, or turn notifications off in your phone settings, at any time.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or payment information. The app has no ads and no third-party analytics.

How we use it

  • To sign you in (we email you a one-time code).
  • To show your reading progress, and to let club administrators see members' reading progress.
  • To send reading reminders.

We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers.

Who processes your data

Your data is stored on our server and database hosting providers, and push notifications are delivered through Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. Bible text is provided by YouVersion. These providers only process data needed to run the app. All data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and check-ins while you are a member. If your account is deleted, your personal details and check-ins are removed from our database.

Deleting your account

To delete your account and its data, email CONTACT_EMAIL from the email address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my BBRC account". We will delete your account, name, email, phone number, check-ins and notification tokens within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Children

BBRC is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
